Tiny Dark Stories

A tiny language model that reads the clock. Its stories are cheerful by day. From 6 in the evening to 6 in the morning, something in them is a little off.

The model's whole prompt

How it works

  • The models were trained on TinyStories with a time header in front of every story. Normal stories got daytime times. 2.7 million spooky twins, written by Gemma from the same names and words, got times between 18:00 and 05:59.
  • Nothing in a spooky story says it is night, so the header is the only clue. The model learns the rule from the digits.
  • Everything runs in your browser with ONNX Runtime on WebAssembly. Nothing you do here is sent anywhere.
